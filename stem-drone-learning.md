# Drone Learning and STEM Education

Drone activities can connect multiple STEM subjects in an engaging way.

## Science

Students can explore:

- Motion
- Forces
- Flight
- Balance
- Air resistance

## Technology

Students can learn about:

- Sensors
- Controllers
- Batteries
- Cameras
- Software

## Engineering

Learners can explore how drone components are designed and how different systems work together.

## Mathematics

Drone-related activities can introduce:

- Distance
- Time
- Speed
- Measurement
- Position

## Project-Based Learning

Students can work on supervised educational activities such as:

- Identifying drone components
- Understanding flight controls
- Creating a basic flight plan
- Studying drone applications
- Designing a drone-related STEM project

The focus should be on understanding concepts and solving problems rather than simply operating a drone.
