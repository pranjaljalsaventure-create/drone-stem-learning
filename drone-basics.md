# Drone Basics for Kids

A drone is an unmanned aircraft that can be controlled remotely or operate using programmed systems.

Learning the basic components of a drone can help children understand how different technologies work together.

## Main Drone Components

### Motors

Motors provide the movement required for the drone to fly.

### Propellers

Propellers generate the lift needed for flight.

### Battery

The battery provides electrical power to the drone.

### Flight Controller

The flight controller processes information and helps manage the drone's movement.

### Sensors

Sensors provide information that can help the drone understand its movement and surroundings.

### Remote Controller

A remote controller allows the operator to send commands to the drone.

## What Students Can Learn

Children can explore:

- Basic drone components
- Flight concepts
- Sensors
- Movement
- Technology applications
- Safe equipment handling

Drone education should introduce concepts gradually according to the learner's age and experience.
