# Drone Safety for Kids

Safety should be the first priority in any children's drone learning activity.

Children should not operate drones independently without appropriate supervision.

## Basic Safety Principles

- Use drones only in suitable locations.
- Follow applicable aviation rules.
- Maintain safe distances from people.
- Keep the drone within the appropriate operating area.
- Inspect the equipment before use.
- Handle batteries carefully.
- Follow instructor or adult guidance.
- Stop operations when conditions become unsafe.

## Pre-Flight Checklist

Before a supervised activity, check:

- Drone condition
- Propellers
- Battery
- Controller
- Flight area
- Weather conditions
- Nearby people and obstacles
- Applicable operating restrictions

## Responsible Learning

Children should also learn about privacy and responsible technology use.

A drone should never be used to invade another person's privacy or to create unsafe situations.

Safety knowledge should be treated as an essential part of drone education rather than an optional topic.
